About Lennoj

Jonnel Alday, known onstage as Lennoj, grew up in Bagong Barrio, Caloocan City, and turned that neighborhood's mix of hip-hop cadence and OPM balladry into his own sound — rap verses that soften into sung hooks about love, distance, and the people who stayed.

Since his 2022 debut single "Telenobela," Lennoj has built a catalog centered on Tagalog storytelling: "Mi Amor" became his breakout, and follow-ups like "Bawal," "Kase Mahal Kita," and "Aking Sinta" carried the same intimate, conversational delivery to a growing audience on Spotify and YouTube.
